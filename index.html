    Every document opens as a document

    Every Wayback link here should replay a PDF or an official issuance: an executive order, proclamation, memorandum, declaration or statement. A hub page, a search page, a 404 shell or a redirect stub does not count. Translations of one document fold into one record that lists its languages. The rule gets checked on a random sample of replays before any count is quoted.

    Dates are upper bounds

    Most documents are dated by the first capture the Wayback Machine made, so they show as by March 2020 and not as a day. Only a date in the URL or in the document itself gets a day. Biden White House actions carry theirs in the URL.

    Moved pages turn into placeholders

    When CDC moved a page, the old address kept answering 200 with a small redirect page, and the crawler kept capturing it. cloth-face-cover.html was 50–57 KB from April to June 2020 and about 1 KB from July. The explorer marks those months and points to the page that replaced it.

    Translations fold into one record

    CDC published many documents in dozens of languages, naming each translation by a suffix: -sp, _ESP, _KO_HR_Korean, -cape-verdean-creole. Each set is one record here, listed under its English file where there is one.

    Trump White House dates come from the live site

    The Trump White House site was moved to trumpwhitehouse.archives.gov on 20 January 2021, so captures there say nothing about when an item was issued. Each item is dated by the first capture of the same address on www.whitehouse.gov, made while it was live. Items first seen before December 2019 are left out.

    Not in yet
